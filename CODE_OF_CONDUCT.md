# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Bookmark Manager Pro welcomes respectful participation from people of every
background and experience level. Project spaces should support constructive
technical discussion, patient collaboration, and learning.

Expected behavior includes:

- Communicating respectfully and assuming good intent while evaluating evidence.
- Giving specific, actionable feedback about work rather than people.
- Respecting privacy, boundaries, identities, and differing levels of experience.
- Accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Keeping discussions relevant to the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing
another person's private information, or encouraging others to engage in such
behavior.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces officially associated with Bookmark Manager Pro. It also
applies when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Until a dedicated private
conduct address is published, submit a confidential report through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/YuraCodedCircuit/Bookmark-Manager-Pro/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with **Conduct report**. Do not include unnecessary personal
information or private conversation history.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, or other contributions that violate this policy. Responses may include
a private warning, temporary participation limits, removal from project spaces,
or a permanent ban, depending on context, impact, and repeated behavior.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Absolute confidentiality or
a particular response time cannot be guaranteed. Retaliation against a person
who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.

## Questions

Non-sensitive questions about this policy may be opened in the
[project issue tracker](https://github.com/YuraCodedCircuit/Bookmark-Manager-Pro/issues).
